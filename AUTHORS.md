## Note on attribution

We do not distinguish between "core" and "external" contributors in
attribution — credit is granted by the contribution itself. If you have
contributed and your name is missing, please open a pull request or an
issue and we will add you.

If you prefer not to appear publicly in this file, you may sign the CLA
under the same terms and ask to be recorded in an internal contributors
register only. Privacy preferences are honoured.
