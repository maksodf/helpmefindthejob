## Consent-first authorship policy

DirectJob Scout lists named contributors only when each contributor has explicitly consented to public attribution. This is the project's standing authorship norm, and it applies to every identity-bearing field — `AUTHORS.md`, [`ACKNOWLEDGMENTS.md`](ACKNOWLEDGMENTS.md), commit-message co-author trailers, and any future contributor roster.

Contributors may choose any of:

1. **Full public attribution** — display name, GitHub handle, role description listed here.
2. **Pseudonymous attribution** — a handle (GitHub, Codeberg, project-specific) with no real name.
3. **Anonymous acknowledgment** — credit in an internal contributors register, no public name or handle.
4. **Full omission** — no record at all, even internally.

The default for a new contributor is **option 1 only if the contributor has affirmatively opted in**. Otherwise the contributor's preference is requested at the time of their first non-trivial contribution and recorded in a private register. We do not name a contributor in this file or in [`ACKNOWLEDGMENTS.md`](ACKNOWLEDGMENTS.md) on the basis of a guess about their preference.

Resolution of any placeholder line in this file (such as the co-maintainer entry above) happens in a **separate dated commit** so the consent event is visible in `git log` as its own line item rather than being folded into an unrelated change. A commit subject of the form `governance: AUTHORS — add <handle> per consent dated YYYY-MM-DD` is the convention.

The policy reflects the project's commitment to contributor autonomy and aligns with the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md), which the project has adopted. It also avoids a class of governance failure where a project bakes a contributor's name into public files without that contributor having said yes — a small but real harm that becomes hard to undo once mirrored, indexed, and forked.

Background: this policy is recorded as Decision 18 in [`docs/grant/04-research-and-decisions.md`](docs/grant/04-research-and-decisions.md), dated 2026-05-17.

## Note on attribution

We do not distinguish between "core" and "external" contributors in
attribution — credit is granted by the contribution itself. If you have
contributed and your name is missing, please open a pull request or an
issue and we will add you.

If you prefer not to appear publicly in this file, you may sign the CLA
under the same terms and ask to be recorded in an internal contributors
register only. Privacy preferences are honoured.
