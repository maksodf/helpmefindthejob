/* MISSION */

  Open-source EU-wide civic employment commons. An MCP-composable
  copilot for users facing structural friction in the European
  labour market — migrants on Anerkennungsverfahren, EU Blue Card
  candidates, Wiedereinstieg returners, career changers, refugees
  on §24 / §4 pathways. Friction-driven, not demographic-driven.

  Persona panel (7): Aïcha · Yusuf · Olga · Mahmoud · Maria ·
  Käthe · Tobias. See /docs/grant/07-personas.md (in repo).

/* SITE */

  Standards: HTML5, CSS3, JSON-LD (schema.org), Apache 2.0
