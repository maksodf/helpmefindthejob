        Save jobs from Indeed, LinkedIn, StepStone & Xing in one click

        When you find a good role on one of those sites today, you probably copy the URL, switch tabs, and paste it somewhere to come back to later. This replaces all that with a single button in your browser's bookmark bar. Drag the button below into the bar once — that's the entire setup. From then on, saving any job is one click: the title, the link, and the source land in your DirectJob queue automatically.

        1. 1
          Make sure your bookmark bar is showing

          It's the row of saved sites under your browser's address bar. If you don't see it, press ⌘+Shift+B on Mac or Ctrl+Shift+B on Windows / Linux to turn it on.

        2. 2
          Drag this button up onto the bar — once

          Click and hold the button below, drag it up onto the bookmark bar, and let go. It now sits there like any other bookmark — but instead of opening a website, clicking it grabs whichever job page you're on and sends it to your queue.

          ↗ Send to DirectJob
        3. 3
          Done. From now on: find a job → click your new bookmark

          On any Indeed, LinkedIn, StepStone or Xing job page, click the "Send to DirectJob" bookmark you just installed. The role lands in your queue with its title, the link back, and a small tag showing which site it came from. You can then score it with AI, draft a cover letter, or apply later — all from one place.
