The job search the labor market wasn’t built for.

Helpmefindthejob captures the specialist HR and bureaucratic knowledge usually locked behind paid consultants — and puts it, free and open, in every job-seeker’s browser. Built for the friction the labor market ignores.

  • EU AI Act compliant by design. Article 12 audit log with HMAC chain, Article 20 portable export, transparency notice shipped — ready for 2 August 2026 enforcement.
  • MCP-composable substrate. 15 tools with JSON-Schema validation, for partner civic agents — housing, healthcare, residency, education.
  • Friction-class rigor. Designed around a seven-persona panel — Aïcha · Yusuf · Olga · Mahmoud · Maria · Käthe · Tobias — with documented bias-testing methodology.
  • The Commons Conservancy stewardship. Federated deployment, no commercial product, Apache 2.0 + CLA, built for civic institutions.
