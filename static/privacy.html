Privacy

Version 1.0 · Last updated 2026-05-09. App version 0.4.0.

This page summarises what Helpmefindthejob does with your data. This text is product-honest, not legal advice. It must be reviewed by counsel before commercial sale.

Privacy-first by default

Helpmefindthejob is a calm tool. We took deliberate decisions to keep it that way:

  • No third-party analytics. No Google Analytics, no Plausible, no Mixpanel, no Hotjar. Your sessions are not heatmapped or session-recorded.
  • No advertising network. We do not sell ads, attribute clicks, or share data with brokers.
  • No surveillance feed. No "engagement" optimisation, no algorithmic ranking that profits from time-on-site, no notifications you didn't ask for.
  • No third-party fonts or scripts at load time. The static surface (sign-in, privacy, terms, Impressum) loads only first-party CSS. No CDN beacons.
  • Strictly necessary cookies only. Two first-party cookies (session + CSRF) and a few language / UI preference keys. No consent banner is required under § 25 TTDSG; see Data retention for the audit trail.
  • Bring-your-own AI. When you click Analyze fit, you choose: paste a session-only key, configure a saved provider, or hand the prompt to your own ChatGPT/Claude subscription. We never resell your prompts to a third party.

What we store

  • Your email address and a salted PBKDF2 hash of your password.
  • Companies you add to your watchlist (name, website, career-page URL, sector, notes).
  • Discovered jobs from public career pages and your scan history (status, errors, pages checked).
  • Imported jobs you keep in your pipeline and the AI-generated brief for each.
  • Your AI provider preferences, but never raw API keys. Session-only API keys you paste in the UI are sent only with one Run AI request and are never written to disk.
  • Operational metadata: session tokens (hashed), last login timestamp, per-day scan/AI usage counters.
  • An admin audit log of admin user-management actions (create, deactivate, role change, password reset, send invite).

What we do not collect

  • Browsing history outside the app.
  • Tracking pixels or third-party analytics.
  • Raw payment data (no payment integration today).

Where data lives

Helpmefindthejob is self-hosted. Your data lives on the operator's server (today: a VPS hosted by the workspace admin). Backups, when configured, are local tarballs on the same host unless the operator configures off-host storage.

External services we may contact on your behalf

  • Public company websites you add (only their public pages, with robots.txt respected).
  • The AI provider you select — only when you explicitly click Analyze fit, Tailor CV, or Prepare brief.
  • An SMTP relay for invitations and password resets, when configured.

Chat assistant — when AI intent routing is enabled

The in-app chat assistant uses two routing layers: slash-commands (e.g. /add-company) and keyword matching — both run locally on our server and never contact a third party.

When the operator enables AI intent classification (set via the server-side HELPMEFINDTHEJOB_CHAT_AI_ROUTER=true + a managed AI key), free-form chat messages that don't match a slash-command or keyword are sent to the operator-configured upstream provider (currently one of: OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, DeepSeek, OpenRouter) for intent classification only. The AI receives the text of your message plus up to the last 6 turns of the conversation; it does not receive your CV, your imported jobs, or any other profile data unless a separate AI feature (Analyze fit / Tailor CV / Prepare brief) is invoked.

The AI never executes anything. It can only suggest one of a fixed whitelist of commands (e.g. add a company, create a saved search); the deterministic confirmation gate still shows you the parsed arguments and asks you to confirm before any database write. Every classification is audit-logged as a chat_ai_route event with the input (truncated to 120 characters) and the classified command — visible to you on request as part of your data export.

You can disable AI routing for your account by switching your AI provider to Manual in Settings; the operator can disable it globally by unsetting HELPMEFINDTHEJOB_CHAT_AI_ROUTER. Per-user rate limits (currently 20 classifications per minute) prevent abuse.

EU AI Act compliance

The EU AI Act (Regulation 2024/1689) becomes enforceable on 2 August 2026. Helpmefindthejob is built to comply from day one because we believe transparency about algorithmic systems is the only acceptable default for a civic-commons tool. The relevant articles for users:

  • Article 12 — Logging / audit trail. Every interaction with the AI features (intent classification, fit scoring, CV tailoring, cover-letter drafting, decision briefs) is recorded in a tamper-evident audit log. The log uses an HMAC chain (each entry signs the previous entry's hash with a per-deployment secret salt), so any after-the-fact modification breaks the chain and is detectable by re-verification. The log schema is documented at compliance/audit-log-schema.md. On request, you can receive your own audit-log entries as part of your portable export (see Article 20 below).
  • Article 13 — Transparency to users. Every AI output in the app is labelled as AI-generated. When a job's fit score is computed by an LLM, the brief explicitly says so and names the model + provider used. When the chat assistant classifies your intent, the audit log captures the classifier decision so you can challenge it. We do not pass off AI output as human judgement.
  • Article 14 — Human oversight. Every action suggested by an AI passes through a deterministic confirmation gate before any database write. You see the parsed parameters and approve them; the AI can never write on your behalf without your explicit confirmation. Documented in compliance/human-oversight-guide.md.
  • Article 20 — Right to portable export. You can export all your data, including AI-call history, audit log entries about you, and your profile, as a single machine-readable archive via Settings → Backup & restore → Export backup (or programmatically via the API). The format is documented; you can re-import into another Helpmefindthejob instance or process with off-the-shelf tools.
  • Article 26 — Deployer obligations. The operator of this Helpmefindthejob instance is the deployer under the AI Act. The deployer is identified on Impressum and is responsible for ensuring the AI features are used in accordance with the Act. Operator obligations are documented at compliance/deployer-operating-manual.md.
  • Article 50 — Transparency for AI-generated content. When the app generates application materials (cover letters, CV tailoring), the output is clearly marked as AI-generated in the UI and in the exported document metadata. You retain editorial control before sending anything to an employer.

Full technical documentation: compliance/ in the source repository. Includes audit-log schema, human- oversight workflow, fundamental-rights impact assessment (FRIA), and the bias-testing methodology used to validate the AI components against the seven-persona panel.

Your rights (GDPR Articles 15–22)

You have the following rights regarding your personal data under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (Regulation 2016/679, "GDPR"). To exercise any of them, contact the operator (see Impressum):

  • Article 15 — Right of access. You may ask for confirmation of whether we process your personal data, and if so, request a copy. Use Settings → Backup & restore → Export backup for an immediate machine-readable export, or ask the operator for a structured copy by email.
  • Article 16 — Right to rectification. You may update inaccurate personal data at any time via the in-app profile settings, or ask the operator to correct anything you cannot edit yourself.
  • Article 17 — Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten"). You may request deletion of your account and all linked personal data. The operator processes erasure requests within 30 days. Note that audit-log entries about completed actions may be retained in pseudonymised form for the legally-required period (currently 180 days) for AI Act Article 12 compliance.
  • Article 18 — Right to restriction of processing. You may request that we hold your data without further processing while a dispute (e.g. accuracy challenge) is resolved.
  • Article 19 — Notification of rectification or erasure. If we have shared your data with anyone (which the self-hosted, no-third-party-analytics design makes rare in practice), we will notify them of any correction or erasure.
  • Article 20 — Right to data portability. Same export endpoint as Article 15, machine-readable, suitable for import into another service. See also the AI Act Article 20 note above (AI-call history is included).
  • Article 21 — Right to object. You may object to processing based on legitimate interests, including any profiling (we do no behavioural profiling, but the right is yours regardless).
  • Article 22 — Right not to be subject to automated decision-making. No fully-automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects are made by Helpmefindthejob. AI features produce suggestions; humans (you) make the decisions. See Article 14 above.

Data Protection Officer (DPO)

Helpmefindthejob's current operator is a small-scale civic deployment that does not meet the GDPR Article 37 threshold for a mandatory Data Protection Officer (no systematic large-scale monitoring; no large-scale processing of special-category data; not a public authority). Privacy questions go to the operator on Impressum. If the deployment grows to a scale that requires a DPO, this section will be updated and the DPO contact published here.
