# Acknowledgments

DirectJob Scout stands on the work of many other projects, communities, and individuals. This file records gratitude for the most direct sources of inspiration, infrastructure, and influence.

## Standards and protocols

- **Model Context Protocol** (MCP) — the composition interface that
  makes DirectJob Scout a piece of civic infrastructure rather than a
  standalone app. We are building one of the first civic-services
  reference implementations of MCP and contribute back to that
  ecosystem.
- **schema.org JobPosting**, **ESCO**, and the **EURES schema** — the
  open employment-data vocabularies that let our exports interoperate
  with the wider European employment-services landscape.
- **Contributor Covenant** — the Code of Conduct we adopt
  ([CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)). Created by Coraline Ada
  Ehmke and licensed under CC BY 4.0; we are grateful for its existence.
- **RFC 9116** — the `/.well-known/security.txt` convention referenced
  in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Institutional influences

- **The Commons Conservancy** — the Dutch stichting whose Programme
  pattern is the institutional shape we are applying for. Their
  multi-tenant legal-entity wrapper is the kind of quiet infrastructure
  that makes ambitious civic projects survivable.
- **NLnet Foundation** — the Next Generation Internet funder we are
  applying to. Their funding model (results-only, milestone-based, no
  progress reports) is what makes small civic projects like this one
  viable.
- **Redwax** — the PKI-infrastructure precedent that demonstrated the
  Apache 2.0 + CLA + Commons Conservancy pattern works for
  infrastructure-shaped open-source projects funded by NLnet. We borrow
  freely from their model.

## Direct technical sources

- Open job-aggregator providers (Adzuna, Indeed public listings,
  LinkedIn public listings, and several country-specific career-page
  patterns) — used in accordance with each provider's terms.
- The **Anerkennung-in-Deutschland** and **IQ-Netzwerk** documentation —
  the canonical references our German bureaucratic-navigation logic is
  modelled on.
- The **Bundesagentur für Arbeit** open data on labor shortages, used
  to calibrate the project's positioning around the 163 German shortage
  occupations.

## Personas and lived-experience input

The five-persona most-acute-use-case panel (Aïcha, Yusuf, Olga, Mahmoud,
Maria) referenced across the project narrative is a composite informed
by maintainer-known testers and by general published research on
EU-mobile and refugee labor-market experience. Two additional
wider-friction-class personas (Käthe, Tobias) joined the panel per
Decision 21 in `docs/grant/04-research-and-decisions.md`; their
composites are informed by published research on returning-after-
caregiving workforce re-entry and on commercial-to-civic-tech career
changes, and they demonstrate that the project's architecture serves
friction structurally rather than serving any single demographic. We
intend to replace fictional personas with consenting anonymised
real-tester stories over time; those stories, once contributed, will
be acknowledged here (or on the contributor's preferred channel).

## How to add an acknowledgment

If we are using your work, your data, or your contribution and you are
not credited here, please open a pull request or an issue. We will
correct the attribution.
