## Outreach discipline

1. **Quality over quantity.** One letter from a respected partner beats five from peripheral contacts. Stop chasing additional letters once one credible one is secured.

2. **Personalise every message.** Generic mail to MBE offices is identifiable in 5 seconds. Open with one specific reference to that office's work (a programme they run, an event they hosted, a press article they appeared in).

3. **Send → wait 7 days → follow up once → stop.** Three unanswered contacts mean the answer is no. Move on without bitterness.

4. **Log every attempt** in the tracker at the top of this document. Date, contact, outcome. If a future agent or partner picks this up, they should not redo your work.

5. **Do not over-promise.** If asked, be honest: "We are early-stage, applying for our first grant. We are not deployed in production at any institution yet. We are looking for one institutional partner willing to support our application now, with a low-effort letter, and explore a pilot deployment after the grant if mutual interest develops."

6. **Honour every offer.** If a partner agrees to support, follow through: send them an updated project status every quarter, invite them to advisory conversations, list them in `ACKNOWLEDGMENTS.md`, mention them in public talks.

## What success looks like

Minimum: **one credible letter of support** in hand by submission day.

Better: **two letters** + the **housing-agent collaboration confirmed** + **one optional academic affiliation pending**.

Best (unlikely in 4 weeks): **three letters** + housing-agent collaboration shipped as Week 2 deliverable + university affiliation confirmed.

We aim for minimum, design for better, and would not be surprised if we miss best.
