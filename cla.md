## How to sign (current process)

Until CLA Assistant or an equivalent tool is wired into the repository's
pull-request workflow, the assent mechanism is:

1. Read this file in full.
2. In Your first non-trivial pull request, add a comment with the
   following exact text:

   > I have read and agree to the DirectJob Scout Individual Contributor
   > License Agreement, version 1.0, dated 2026-05-17.

3. State Your full legal name and the email address You commit from.
4. A maintainer records the assent in `AUTHORS.md` (or in an internal
   contributors register) before merging Your contribution.

Trivial contributions (typo fixes, documentation clarifications,
single-line bug fixes) may be merged without prior CLA assent at
maintainer discretion. Anything beyond trivial requires assent on file
first.

## Why an individual CLA at all?

The CLA preserves the project's ability to relicense the codebase later if
a strategic reason arises (for example, dual-licensing for a specific
institutional integration, or moving to a different OSI-approved license
on collective maintainer decision). Without a CLA on file from each
contributor, that decision would require contacting every past
contributor individually — an operation that scales poorly. This pattern
is used by Redwax, by the Apache Software Foundation, and by many other
projects of similar shape.

The CLA does **not**:

- transfer copyright ownership away from You
- prevent You from using Your Contribution in any other project
- restrict Your right to fork DirectJob Scout under Apache 2.0
- grant the Project rights beyond what is needed to distribute Your
  Contribution under the Project's license

If You are not comfortable with the CLA terms, You are still welcome to
file issues, comment on pull requests, suggest changes in discussions, or
fork the project. The CLA is only required for contributions that the
Project will merge into the canonical codebase.
